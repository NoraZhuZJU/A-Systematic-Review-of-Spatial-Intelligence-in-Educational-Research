# A-Systematic-Review-of-Spatial-Intelligence-in-Educational-Research
A Systematic Review of Spatial Intelligence in Educational Research from 2021 to 2026.（ICRAI 2026 Conference）

# Abstract
Spatial computing connects digital representations with the objects, bodies, and environments involved in learning. This systematic review examines its educational purposes, technologies, and organizational forms across 62 reports retrieved from Web of Science, Scopus, and IEEE Xplore for January 2021–15 February 2026. Expanded content coding identifies four dominant purposes: conceptual/spatial understanding (22 reports), procedural rehearsal (21), exploration/creation (13), and assessment support (6). AR/MR is the principal delivery form (29). Relationship mapping shows that formal education combines conceptual understanding and rehearsal, organized non-formal training emphasizes rehearsal, and informal public learning emphasizes exploration and creation. Technologies connect with disciplinary tasks through inspectable representations, situated action, shared reference, and movement-based feedback. A grounding–action–feedback–transfer framework integrates these patterns and proposes a basis for designing future spatial learning environments. The synthesis prioritizes task-relevant fidelity, teacher orchestration, and assessment of competence after spatial support is withdrawn.
